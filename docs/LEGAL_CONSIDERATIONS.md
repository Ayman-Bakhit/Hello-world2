# Legal Considerations

I am not a lawyer; this is a checklist of questions for qualified counsel **before any public launch**: money transmission, custody, securities, commodities, broker/dealer, token issuance, creator fee routing, charitable solicitation (state registrations), AML/KYC, sanctions screening, state requirements, tax reporting, privacy.

Design stance: avoid custody; user-controlled vaults; creator fee routing to charity may itself raise solicitation and money-transmission questions, get an opinion early because it shapes the architecture.

Marketing bans: tax avoidance, tax loophole, guaranteed write-off, guaranteed profits, risk-free, safe crypto, anti-rug as a guarantee. Enforced by `BANNED_PHRASES` test in shared.
