                                   What is PECOS?
 The Provider Enrollment, Chain, and Ownership System, or PECOS, is
 CMS’s online Medicare enrollment management system for providers
 and suppliers.
 Use PECOS to:
 Manage your Medicare Enrollment:
         ✓ Submit an initial enrollment application
         ✓ Revalidate your enrollment information
         ✓ Update enrollment information
         ✓ Withdraw or reactivate an enrollment
         ✓ Report a change of ownership
 Complete other Actions:
         ✓ Add or terminate reassignments
         ✓ Track application, revalidation, and enrollment status

          Why Applications Are Developed for Additional Information:


          Missing Signatures
                 • Application not e-signed, or
                 • Missing certification statement upload
          Missing Supporting Documentation
                 • EIN verification
                 • Voided Check or Bank Letterhead
                 • Diploma/Transcripts
                 • DEA/License/Certification
          Missing Education Information
                 • School of graduation not provided

                                       Top PECOS Tips

             Let AOs/DOs Know a Signature Request Is Coming
                • After submission, AOs/DOs may receive a PECOS
                  certification/signature request. Delays in completing required
                  signatures can delay processing.
             Have Supporting Documentation Ready
                • Upload required documents with the application whenever
                  possible, such as EIN verification and other requested supporting
                  documentation.
              Review the Application Before Submission
                • Double-check that all sections are complete and accurate before
                  submitting.
              Monitor Email After Submission
                • Watch for development requests and respond promptly to avoid
                  processing delays.
                • Be sure to check your Spam/Junk folder regularly for CMS or
                  contractor communications.
