**Best candidate:** a remote service for very small USDA-inspected meat processors. For 30 days I'd chase customers who haven't sent cut sheets (the forms where they say how they want their animal cut) and customers who haven't picked up finished meat. It's phone and text only, with no food handling.
- Evidence the problem exists: processors publish rules like "if no cut sheet, we cut to the butcher's recommendation" ([Western Heritage](https://westernheritagemeatco.squarespace.com/cut-sheets)).
- Evidence anyone would pay an outsider to fix it: none yet.
- My confidence it's a real paid business: about 20 to 30%. That's a judgment call, not a calculation.

**Strongest evidence against:**

- Plants already push the cost of missing cut sheets onto farmers through default cuts, so they may not feel enough pain to pay.
