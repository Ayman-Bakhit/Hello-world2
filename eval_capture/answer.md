It uses provider data, not patient data, so there's no patient-record access. CMS has an approved way for an outside person to work on a practice's file (called a "surrogate" connection), and you never get the practice's login. I found no state license requirement, but that check was partial.

**Strongest reasons it could fail:**

- It's a big trust ask for someone with no track record.

- It's a labor-bound service, not equity-scale wealth.
