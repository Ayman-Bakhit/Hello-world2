## Strongest disconfirming evidence

1. **Price anchor.** MedSole charges $99 per application, after approval, with revalidation included. Many practices also get this bundled from their billing company.
2. **It happens once every 5 years.** The recurring work (re-attesting each provider's CAQH profile, which insurers check, every 120 days per CAQH's FAQ (Unverified, search snippet only); change reporting; recredentialing with private insurers) is real, but buyers may not pay a monthly fee for it.
3. **Trust gap.** You'd be a newcomer touching their Medicare billing status. A mistake could cost the practice money.
4. **Prospect-specific:** a possible UnityPoint affiliation could make the first prospect a non-buyer.

## Test with a stop rule

- **Cost:** about $100 for the domain and email at the start. Insurance and the LLC only once someone says yes.
- **Time:** about 65 hours.

**Steps:**
1. **Days 1-7:** learn the PECOS revalidation and Identity & Access steps from CMS materials (about 15 hours). Pull the 3,216 groups and pick 150 due in 60-150 days, Iowa and neighboring states first.
2. **Days 8-30:** reach each group by phone first, then email if they give an address, with 2 follow-ups each.
3. **Track:** qualified conversations, "billing company covers it" answers, and paid invoices.

**Stop or redirect if any of these happen:**
- Fewer than 3 qualified conversations after 150 groups contacted.
- More than 50% of the "no" replies say their billing company handles it.
- Zero paid by day 45.

If you stop, take the credentialing-company job instead. **Continue** only if at least 1 paid and at least 1 accepts the retainer.
